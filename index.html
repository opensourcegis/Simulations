Simulation-based training for surveyors.

Training simulators from the Photogrammetry & Remote Sensing division, National Institute for Geo-informatics Science & Technology (NIGST). Everything runs in the browser — nothing to install, no accounts, and your progress stays on your own device.

Drone Mission Planner

Plan a photogrammetry survey over live map imagery. Every control starts at zero — pick a launch site, fight the wind, mind the masts and the 120 m ceiling, and land with battery to spare. The review board only scores a committed plan, and a bad one is flown to its conclusion.

Survey Mission Designer

Five taskings, five terrains, one review board. Digitize a 2–3 km² block on live imagery, match the payload — nadir, oblique or LiDAR — to the deliverable, and plan around real OSM obstructions and real SRTM terrain. Fixed-wing gets one flight; multirotor gets a battery budget; the hills tax everyone.
