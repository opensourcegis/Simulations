Simulation-based training for surveyors.

Training simulators from the Photogrammetry & Remote Sensing division, National Institute for Geo-informatics Science & Technology (NIGST). Everything runs in the browser — nothing to install, no accounts, and your progress stays on your own device.

Survey Mission Designer

Five taskings, five terrains, one review board. Plan a fixed 2 km² block on live imagery, match the payload — nadir, oblique or LiDAR — to the deliverable, and plan around real OSM obstructions, real SRTM terrain and the forecast wind. One battery per mission; the hills and the headwind tax everyone.

Aerial Triangulation Lab

Three overlapping frames, rough navigation, and a block that won't map itself. Measure tie points by hand across the stereo overlaps, respect the von Gruber pattern, then run the bundle adjustment and watch the cameras swing into place. Scored on ray residuals, coverage and checkpoint QA — blunders included.

LiDAR Scan Patterns

Six real beam-steering mechanisms — oscillating mirror, rotating polygon, Palmer conical, Risley prism, MEMS and rotating multi-beam. Pick one, fly the drone over the map, and watch its ground pattern build up in slow motion: zig-zags, parallel lines, ellipses, rosettes and rings, coloured by elevation or scan sequence.

Beam & Target Interaction

Where does a laser pulse actually go? Watch it split into reflection, absorption, transmission and scatter on water, grass, black asphalt, glass and white roofs — and see how only a few parts per million ever find the receiver. Then push a pulse through a small tree for first, intermediate and last returns.

True Ortho-Rectification

Why do buildings lean in an aerial photo, and where do their façades go in a true orthophoto? Probe relief displacement point by point, link the image to a DTM or DSM, and project it onto the datum with a real rectifier — watch roofs snap onto their footprints, façades vanish, and occlusions get filled from a second photo.
